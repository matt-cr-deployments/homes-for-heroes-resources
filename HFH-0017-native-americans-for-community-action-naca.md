### What ACMF reported

*This section is ACMF's own words and ACMF's own answers. Nothing here was added or guessed.*

Native Americans for Community Action (NACA) helps veterans by providing specialized medical care, behavioral health support, and benefit coordination services. 

Specialized Health & Wellness
Veterans Choice Program: NACA accepts "Veterans Choice Cards," allowing eligible veterans to receive medical care at NACA Family Health Center rather than a distant VA facility.
Veteran-Specific Therapy: Offers individual, couples, and family psychotherapy tailored to military culture. This includes treatment for PTSD, readjustment issues, anxiety, and depression.
Behavioral Health Expertise: The center employs therapists specifically experienced in working with veterans from all service branches (Soldiers, Marines, Sailors, Airmen, and Reservists). 

Benefits & Financial Support
Benefit Coordination: Dedicated staff assist veterans in navigating and applying for VA benefits and other health insurance options like AHCCCS (Medicaid).
Supportive Services: Veterans may qualify for the Supportive Services Program, which provides one-time annual financial assistance for essential needs such as rent, utilities, food, and clothing.
Sack Lunch Program: Provides free daily meals to community members, including veterans, on a first-come, first-served basis.
