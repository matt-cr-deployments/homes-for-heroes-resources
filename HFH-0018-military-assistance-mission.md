*This section is ACMF's own words and ACMF's own answers. Nothing here was added or guessed.*

For Arizona Veterans post 9/11. Active, Reserve, Guard or was an active duty Veteran. Offer 1 time financial assistance. Most assistance is for rent or utilities. Must have Honorable or General DC. E-5 or below or Purples Heart recipients. For veterans who were active duty. Can provide financial assistance for veterans and the families of veterans for housing. They raise money from fund raising events,   donations. Turnaround time for approval for eligible veteran is 24-48 hours. Legal history is not necessarily a barrier to eligibility.
