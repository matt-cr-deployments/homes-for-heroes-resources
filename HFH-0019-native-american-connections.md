### What ACMF reported

*This section is ACMF's own words and ACMF's own answers. Nothing here was added or guessed.*

Has 3 apartment properties with Veteran's preference. Offer permanent supportive housing to Veterans with case manager. Accepts section 8/HUD VASH vouchers. They can provide bus passes, work with SMI case managers. They work with the food banks for those facing food insecurity. Can help apply for SSA benefits. Have a wellness clinic and Patina SUD treatment center they can refer out to if needed. Have a mobile medical clinic that visits sites. They receive referrals from Dept of corrections however unable to accommodate those with a sex offense.

- **Hours:** Mon-Fri 8am-5pm
- **Who it serves:** Anyone, regardless of connection to the Military, Service members (currently serving), Families of service members (currently serving), Former service members/veterans and Families of former service members/veterans
- **Gender:** Any gender
- **Can someone refer themselves?** Yes
- **Can it be used anonymously?** No
- **Transport provided?** Sometimes
- **Topics ACMF filed it under:** Finances & Benefits, Housing & Homelessness, Mental Health & Substance Use and Physical Health
- **Sub-topics:** Affordable housing programs, Budgeting, Bus passes, Food, General mental health services and Permanent supportive housing

**Cost.** ACMF reported this resource as discounted or subsidised.

### Before you refer someone here

**This directory never states a level of care.** ACMF's form never asked for one, so nobody can say from this record whether a program is detox, residential, outpatient, or sober living. The words above are the words in the description. A veteran in acute withdrawal sent to a sixty-day residential program that will not admit anyone still using is the exact harm this rule exists to prevent. **Call intake and ask.**
