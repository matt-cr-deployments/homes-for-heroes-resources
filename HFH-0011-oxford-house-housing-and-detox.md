### What ACMF reported

*This section is ACMF's own words and ACMF's own answers. Nothing here was added or guessed.*

Oxford House is a self-governing and cost-effective sober living environment that can aid in the transition of individuals following treatment and offer peer-based support and behavior modeling for drug and alcohol abstinence. Oxford House leases the house and then the members in the house all pay into the lease. (Over 4400 houses nationwide) 140 around the valley. Can apply online and will shoot that application to every house of that gender. Once they apply they go through the list of houses and reach out to that house to get an interview set up. Acceptance is immediate and they can move in right away. Peer supported model. The males and females of the house will give or guide them with basic needs with "Welcome Home" packages and not going without. They also have a community of clothing donations. They create "contracts" to get mental health appts, take meds, seek employment and help each other with referrals, resources and support. Their website is updated in real time (anyone can search for a home and apply). Entry into the program/housing is same day or next day.
