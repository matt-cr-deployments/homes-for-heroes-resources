### What ACMF reported

*This section is ACMF's own words and ACMF's own answers. Nothing here was added or guessed.*

The Serenity Complex in Yuma, Arizona, is a transitional housing program operated by Crossroads Mission. It is designed specifically to help men who have completed a recovery program or have been recently released from jail or prison transition back into the community. 

Mission:
The mission of the Serenity Complex is to provide a supervised and structured, drug- and alcohol-free environment where men can continue their recovery process. The program focuses on helping participants acquire the skills necessary for long-term success and independent living.

Accessing the Resource: 
Access typically begins with an initial screening to ensure the participant is prepared for a supervised, sober living environment. 
Initial Point of Entry: Many participants start at the First Step Center (944 S. Arizona Ave) for medically monitored detox and stabilization before moving to the Serenity Complex.
Referral Pathways: While you can self-refer, the program frequently accepts formal referrals from partners like the Yuma Regional Medical Center, Yuma County Adult Probation, and local law enforcement.
Intake Contact: You can initiate the process 24/7 by calling the toll-free help line at (602) 780-1442 or the direct facility line at (928) 783-5642.
Required Documentation: At intake, you must provide valid, non-expired identification and pass a breathalyzer or drug screening to demonstrate immediate sobriety.
