# Resources in Phoenix, Arizona

*Every resource ACMF filed with an address in Phoenix, in alphabetical order. The town is the one ACMF reported. A resource based in another town is not listed in the first section, even if it serves the whole state. For distances, use the ZIP code pages. **This directory never states a level of care.** A sentence quoted below is what ACMF's description says, not a confirmed service; call intake and ask before referring.*

ZIP codes of the resources in Phoenix: 85012, 85014, 85015, 85016, 85020, 85031, 85036.

## In Phoenix

- **2 LUV 1 Another** — 2324 W Turney Ave, 85015 · (602) 975-6859. ACMF's description says: "Transitional housing, 600 beds men and women, standard CM  refer out for CBI,  allows individuals with sex offense history, mostly level 1-2 , level 3 on case by case basis, work with Probation, parole. … Must be ADL independent, some home health allowed. … Refer out for mental health treatment, detox and acute SUD treatment. … Private pay and securing work is required."
- **Calvary Healing Center** — 720 E Montebello Ave., 85014 · (520) 457-0064. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "Has a detox program, medication assisted treatment and RNs available."
- **Cornerstone Healing Center** — 4344 W Indian School Rd. #22, 85031 · (480) 334-7275. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "They are unable to accommodate those with a sex offense history. … Offers residential addiction and mental health treatment, partial hospitalization program, IOP, outpatient, virtual IOP, supportive housing, recovery coaching. … They have a medical detox center."
- **House of Refuge** — 9844 N 7th place, 85020 · (602) 678-0223. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "Faith based program with up to 12 months of supportive housing. … Has a single men, single women, single mothers with children program."
- **Military Assistance Mission** — 1428 E Northern Ave, 85020 · (602) 246-6429. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "Must have Honorable or General DC. … Has some education scholarship and assistance programs and employers they partner with to hire Veterans."
- **Native American Connections** — 3216 N 3rd St., 85012 · (602) 254-3247. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "Have a wellness clinic and Patina SUD treatment center they can refer out to if needed. … They receive referrals from Dept of corrections however unable to accommodate those with a sex offense. … Accepts section 8/HUD VASH vouchers. … Offer permanent supportive housing to Veterans with case manager."
- **Recovery Empowerment Network** — 212 E Osborn Rd., 85012 · (602) 248-0368. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "Provides mental health and SUD treatment services to adults ages 18-25. … Has a crisis diversion skills program called Hope's Door. … Has a recovery center that holds many groups, PTSD support group, SMART recovery, Self Esteem group, Women's group, Art and Music etc."
- **STAR Stand Together and Recover Centers** — 77 E Columbus Ave, 85012 · (602) 231-0071. ACMF's description says: "Has a 90 day wraparound service program for those discharging from an inpatient psychiatry facility."
- **Sojourner Center** — PO BOX 20156, 85036 · (602) 244-0997. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "120 day shelter, 24 hour staff for victims of DV, human trafficking, sexual assault."
- **Vogue Recovery Center** — 4122 N 17th St., 85016 · (855) 628-2899. ACMF listed it as serving the whole state. ACMF's description says: "Can accept those with TBI. … They use a variety of therapies and treatment approaches including medication Assisted Treatment (MAT). … Treatment center that provides medical detox, residential treatment, partial hospitalization program, IOP, outpatient program, sober living, Veterans program, chronic pain program and alumni program."
